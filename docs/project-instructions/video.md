## Final Video

Your team will produce a video presenting your project, **2 minutes maximum**.

**The video should stand on its own: someone who has never seen your project should come away understanding who it's for, what need it addresses, and how it works.**

Show your actual coded, high-fidelity design in use, running on a real phone or whatever device your design targets. Most videos combine voice-over with screen captures and live footage of the design being used in its real context. Focus on one or two of your primary tasks. Unlike PROG 01 and PROG 02 we are not looking for a narration through various screens of your design. We need to see it in context of use, so in a real world setting, when there are moments of interaction, you will certainly want to focus on some of the screen interactions, so it's appropriate to navigate through some of those more closely, but it should feel like we're in the actual context of use with real users, not you walking us through all of the screens.

You'll likely show a short segment of this video during your team's presentation on December 8. We'll also play the videos at the project showcase. The video is graded separately from your presentation and report, as a standalone piece of your project.

**Requirements**

- **2 minutes maximum. This is a hard limit.**
- **Landscape only. No vertical video!**

**How to submit**

1.  Add your video to the **CS160 Fall 2026 YouTube Playlist**: <https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLFZRxP6RGLHg&jct=fdzCo7XwCcnQWZQLI6a8eA> \
    Set the video to public or unlisted so it plays in the playlist.
2.  Upload your video to bCourses.

**Grading**

- Does it stand alone as a representation of the project?
- Was the video within the 2-minute limit?
- Did it capture the right context of use for your users?
- Was it well narrated, shot, and edited?
- Did it show the actual working design in use on a phone or the target device?
- Did it make good use of sound and visual elements?
