## Public Showcase

**Thursday, December 10, 2:00–4:00 PM**\
**Gateway, first-floor public area**

**You will be the inaugural class to hold a public showcase in our fancy new Gateway building!!!**

The semester ends with a public showcase of your work. Faculty, students, the CDSS, and CS community, and invited guests will come through to see what you've designed.

**Attendance is required.** Every team member must be at the team's poster for the **entire showcase**, from 2:00 to 4:00 PM, ready to present the project and answer questions. Be there at 2:00 sharp.

**Your poster will be printed and set up for you on an easel.** That's all that will be provided: **no table and no power.**

**Come prepared to:**

- **Give your pitch at two lengths:** a 30-second version for people passing by, and a 2–3 minute version for people who stop to talk.
- **Demo your working prototype (optional, but encouraged).** If you'd like visitors to try it, bring it on a phone, tablet, or laptop. Since there's no table or power, plan to hold your devices, charge them fully beforehand, and bring a battery pack if you have one. Have your demo loaded and ready to go. Don't count on fast Wi-Fi.
- **Explain your process.** Visitors often ask who your users are, what you learned from them, and how your design changed along the way.

Talk with as many visitors as you can. The showcase is a chance to share your work beyond the class, and it's good practice presenting design work to people who have never seen it before.
