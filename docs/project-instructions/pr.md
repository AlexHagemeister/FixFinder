Your project will be featured at the CS 160 public showcase on **December 10**. We’ll use what you submit here to promote your work to the public: on the showcase website, in announcements, and in printed materials. Write for a general audience, not for course staff.

**One person per team submits the form.** You must sign in with your Berkeley account. You can come back to the form and edit your response at any time before the deadline.

**What to submit**

1.  **Team number**
2.  **Team members.** The full name of every teammate, one per line, exactly as it should appear in public materials.
3.  **Project title**
4.  **Tagline.** One line, **100 characters max** including spaces, that says what your project is or does. Example: *“Hands-free handoffs for home care aides at the end of a shift.”*
5.  **Project description.** **150 words max.** Explain who your project is for, what need it addresses, and what it does. Avoid course jargon and technical detail. Write it so a visitor at the showcase would want to come see it.
6.  **Representative image.** One image that shows your project at its best, ideally your design in use in its real context.
    - **16:9 landscape**, at least **1920 × 1080 pixels**
    - JPG or PNG, under 10 MB
    - Avoid text-heavy slides or bare screenshots on a white background. A good photo of the design in use is best.

The form won’t accept a tagline over 100 characters or a description over 150 words.
